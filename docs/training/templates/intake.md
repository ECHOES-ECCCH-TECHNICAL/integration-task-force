# Intake assessment comment

Use this template on the existing training Resource issue. That issue is the Training Integration Unit (TIU); do not open an assessment issue. Copy **one** of the two comment templates below: the full Annex 2 assessment for intended Levels 2–3, or the basic listing assessment for Level 1. Both routes require the completed Candidate Card.

Replace every bracketed prompt. For criteria, replace `Pending` with `Met`, `Condition`, `Not met`, or `Not applicable`. Give evidence and explain every condition or non-applicable criterion. In this template, the deliverable's “learning objectives” are recorded as learning outcomes in the Candidate Card. The full assessment retains the wording of [D5.2, Annex 2](https://zenodo.org/records/20442259), including its reference to “Virtual Applications”.
