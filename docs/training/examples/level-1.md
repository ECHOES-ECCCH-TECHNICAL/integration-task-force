## 4. Learning purpose

### Concrete use case or identified need

A museum educator needs to share a workshop handout with another institution. Its current description does not identify the audience, expected learning outcomes, workload or reuse conditions, making it difficult for colleagues to judge its suitability.

### Learning goals

Help heritage staff describe learning resources clearly enough for colleagues to assess relevance and plan reuse. The guide introduces a small set of practical descriptive elements and shows how they work together in a completed example. Its purpose is to improve discovery and informed selection across institutions, without assuming a particular training platform or requiring learners to master a metadata standard.

### Learning outcomes

- Identify the audience, learning purpose and estimated workload of a training resource.
- Write two observable learning outcomes for an existing handout.
- Record a resource's provider, link, language and stated reuse conditions in a concise description.
