## 3. Learners

### Target audiences

Training coordinators and heritage project staff adapting an existing institutional workshop for reuse by another organisation.

Intermediate

### Prerequisites and assumed knowledge

Experience organising or delivering one heritage training session. Learners should bring an outline or handout they are authorised to adapt; an original fictional sample will be supplied for learners without their own material.

### Estimated learner effort

About 3 hours, including a 90-minute planning exercise and a 30-minute self-review.

## 4. Learning purpose

### Concrete use case or identified need

A sister-project training team wants another institution to reuse its workshop format. The outline needs explicit outcomes, learner assumptions, facilitation guidance and an evaluation method so a new facilitator can deliver it independently.

### Learning goals

Enable heritage training coordinators to turn an existing workshop outline into a reusable learning session. The module connects audience analysis, outcomes, activities, workload and evaluation in one practical design process. A worked example and planning worksheet help learners make the assumptions behind their session explicit. The intended contribution to an ECHOES trainer-development pathway is shared pedagogical practice, with periodic review of relevance and teaching quality.

### Learning outcomes

- Define the intended audience and prerequisites for a reusable workshop.
- Align three observable learning outcomes with activities and an appropriate workload.
- Produce a facilitation outline that another trainer can use without a separate briefing.
- Select a proportionate method for checking learning outcomes and collecting feedback.
